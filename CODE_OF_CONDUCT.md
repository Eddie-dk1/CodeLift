# CodeLift Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in CodeLift a respectful, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different levels of experience.
- Accept feedback gracefully and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private information found in reports, logs, fixtures, and security disclosures.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring contributors to share proprietary code, credentials, or identifying data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Temporary or permanent participation restrictions
may be applied when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Eddie-dk1). Security vulnerabilities should instead follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, respect the reporter's privacy where possible, and
take action proportionate to the behavior and its impact.
